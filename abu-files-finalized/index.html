ABOUT US

Our Story

Established in 1987, Abu Files & Stationery has grown into a trusted manufacturer and wholesale supplier of files, folders, diaries and stationery products.

We serve hospitals, offices, companies, schools, colleges, corporate organizations and other institutions across Tamil Nadu. Along with standard products, we manufacture customized products according to customer requirements.

From hospital record files and school folders to office files, diaries, pouches and stationery, our experienced team works closely with customers to understand specifications and deliver practical products for their needs.
